Welcome Message

Welcome

I would like to welcome you to the University to take part in this Short Course. As you can see from the resources in this microsite, an undergraduate degree requires you to become an independent learner and we want to see you take ownership of your work and your time. Be prepared to work collaboratively with your peers and approach your studies in a respectful, responsible manner.

You will be encouraged to engage in self-evaluation as this helps us to achieve our best. It will allow you to track the development of key skills, be responsive to feedback, and gain confidence in asking questions and discussing.

The aim of this course is to give you the space to reflect upon your learning so far, and to see how small, incremental changes to how you think and how you act can significantly influence your engagement with your subject, and also your enjoyment of learning. Ultimately, research has show that students who can become reflective learners tend to perform better academically than peers that chose not to.

Staff are here this week to help and guide you through the short course and we aim to provide an enriching and fulfilling experience that enables you to achieve your goals. The more you put into this programme, the more rewarding it will be, so engage fully, be open to new experiences, and take advantage of every opportunity. Most of all, enjoy your experience at Hope this week!

Dr Glen Pennington

Short Course Leader
